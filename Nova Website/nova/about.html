Our Story

Spreading the message of the Quran, globally

We are continually seeking new methods to convey the mission of Islam. Through the dedication of our Quran services, hundreds of thousands of individuals in Pakistan and many other regions of the world have gained proficiency in Quran recitation. Nova Online Quran Academy is fulfilling its responsibility to spread Islamic teachings globally, with the primary aim of educating people of all ages worldwide regarding the teachings of the Quran and Sunnah.

We have developed several innovative teaching techniques that motivate students and make learning the Quran online with qualified tutors engaging and effective. In every session we track progress through assigned homework and conduct evaluations to ensure students advance through each lesson with instant feedback from experienced instructors.
